## User story

As **Rory**, I want **live yards to the green** on mapped FWY holes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Readout only in FWY with surveyed coords.
2. Hidden on unmapped holes and in SIM.
3. Haversine to green pin in whole yards.
4. Stream pauses when leaving tab or switching mode.
